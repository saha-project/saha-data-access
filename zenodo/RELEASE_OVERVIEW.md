**Donors:** the cohort has 120 donor cores: 100 from SAHA and 20 from Dulai IBD.
114 of them have data; 6 were never acquired or fall outside the acquired FOVs.
Sex is recorded as 55 M, 38 F and 27 unknown. Donor identity is stored per cell in
`donor_core`, because one acquisition can hold several donors. The unit to count
is the **measurement** (one core in one acquisition), and there are 327 of them.

**Format:** SpatialData zarr / h5ad with stitched morphology images, a public
obs whitelist (63 columns) and CC-BY 4.0.

**Before v1 can ship** (full list in `STATUS.md`):
- The PRCA build (3 prostate cancer acquisitions) is unconfirmed.
- The Dulai obs whitelist needs to be re-applied.
- Packaging has to run once, after the two items above.
- Three items in `record_metadata.json` are unresolved: ORCIDs, funders and the embargo date.

## astroSTAMP — deferred

This is peripheral blood from the Inspiration4 and Polaris Dawn astronauts (crew C001,
C005 and C006). It was sampled pre-launch (L-194/-44/-21), in flight (FD-4) and after
return (R+1/+39). The data is 4,855,308 cells × 4,826 genes, with Azimuth PBMC labels.

It uses the same schema as v1: `donor_core` is the spot and `donor_patient` is
the crew member. Timepoints are kept as separate cores. The build path already exists
(`export_seurat.R` → `build_from_seurat.py`).

**What gates it:** the crew are a few publicly identifiable people, so the Phase 0
approvals do not cover them. NASA/TRISH and crew consent terms are needed, and the
access tier and licence must be chosen on purpose rather than inherited from v1.
Two more questions are open: whether it ships as its own record set (recommended),
and where the Xenium STAMP data (slides X-D, X-E) is.
