## PBMC STAMP — controlled access, deferred

Peripheral blood from healthy adult donors, profiled by CosMx WTx STAMP
(18,935 RNA targets + 50 negative probes; 36 spots on 3 flowcells; 3,220,395 cells)
and by Xenium 5K STAMP (4,826 of the 5,001 panel genes; 24 spots on 2 slides).
The Xenium h5ad's 4,855,308 cells are not a unique-cell count (each spot is split
into "slide1"/"slide2" halves, and 868,975 cells carry no spot). Its provenance is
being checked with St Jude.

Release labels are grouped so that individual donors cannot be singled out:
donors are `SAHA_D200`–`SAHA_D208`, assigned in shuffled order; every released
draw has `timepoint_group = baseline`; no cohort or timepoint labels are
released. The sample table is `zenodo/stamp/stamp_release_samples.csv` (not
committed). The analysis key is in `private/`.

**What gates it:** consent and data-sharing terms for this cohort, then a
deliberately chosen access tier (controlled, DUA), not the v1 CC-BY default.
It ships as its own record set.
